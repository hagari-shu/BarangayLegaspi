# Resident Account Approval Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

## 1. Eligibility

Accounts are intended for residents of Barangay Legaspi, Tayug, Pangasinan. Each resident should maintain only one account. A valid mobile number is required and must be unique to the account.

## 2. Required Information

Registration requires the resident's:

- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Address
- Zone and household information when requested by an administrator

The barangay may request additional proof of residency when information cannot be verified from existing records.

## 3. Approval Process

1. Every new resident account starts with `Pending Verification` status.
2. The resident cannot access protected web app features while pending.
3. An authorized Staff member reviews the submitted information in the Staff Portal.
4. Staff changes the account to `Active Resident` when approved.
5. Staff may reject the account when eligibility or information cannot be verified.
6. Administrators have read-only monitoring access. Break-glass access never permits account approval, rejection, or profile changes.

## 4. Access Rules

- Pending accounts cannot sign in to the web app or submit service requests.
- Approved accounts may use resident services and submit requests.
- Rejected, suspended, or deactivated accounts cannot use protected services.
- Staff may review pending resident registrations only for official barangay work.
- Administrators may monitor account status but cannot make account changes, including during an emergency grant.

## 5. Verification Timeline

The barangay should review a complete registration within 1–3 business days. Delays may occur when information requires manual verification or the barangay office is closed.

## 6. Rejected Accounts

An account may be rejected for inaccurate information, duplicate registration, ineligibility, or inability to verify residency. Staff should tell the resident how to correct the information and request another review.

## 7. Privacy and Security

- Resident information must not appear on public pages or public exports.
- Mobile numbers, email addresses, addresses, household details, and location data are restricted to authorized users.
- Passwords must be stored only as secure hashes.
- Staff approval and rejection decisions are recorded for audit purposes.
- Information should be collected only for a stated barangay service or administrative purpose.

## 9. Appeals

Residents may contact the Barangay Legaspi office to appeal a rejection, correct inaccurate information, or ask about a delayed review. Authorized Staff should review the appeal and record the final decision.
