# Location Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

## Purpose

Barangay Legaspi may use a resident's location only to support barangay services, verify the resident's assigned zone, and coordinate an active service or emergency request.

## Consent

- Location sharing is optional and requires the resident's clear consent before collection.
- The application must explain why location is requested before the browser permission prompt appears.
- A resident can refuse or revoke location permission without losing access to their account.

## What Is Collected

- The application may collect latitude, longitude, collection time, and the resident's assigned zone.
- Live or continuous tracking is not collected by default.
- Location is collected only when the resident starts an approved location-based workflow.

## Who Can See It

- Residents can see their own saved location and consent status.
- Authorized staff and administrators can see location only when it is needed for a service, verification, or emergency response.
- Location must not be exposed in public pages, public exports, or to other residents.

## Storage and Retention

- Location records must be stored with the related user or request and protected by the application's access controls.
- Location records should be deleted when the related workflow is closed, unless a longer retention period is required by law or an active records policy.
- Administrators must be able to remove a saved location when a resident withdraws consent.

## Accuracy and Safety

- Location data is advisory and may be inaccurate, unavailable, or outdated.
- Staff must not use location as the sole basis for denying a service or taking disciplinary action.
- Emergency services must not rely on this application as a replacement for a direct emergency call.

## Implementation Requirement

Before location collection is enabled, the application must provide:

1. A consent checkbox or equivalent explicit action.
2. A visible explanation of the purpose and retention period.
3. A way to view, revoke, and delete the saved location.
4. Role-based protection for every location endpoint and screen.
5. Audit records for staff and administrator access to location data.
